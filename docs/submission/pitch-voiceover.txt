KillSwitch Wallet. Agent spend controls on Arbitrum.

The problem: an LLM that can pay will overspend or pay the wrong merchant. Prompts are not a control plane. There is no hard budget, no allowlist, and no kill switch.

The solution is a Session Key. You grant a budget, a merchant allowlist, and a deadline — not your master key. The agent only proposes. SessionPolicy guards every payment on-chain.

User grants. Agent proposes. The contract executes or denies. Then you freeze or close.

Payment Denied is success. Over budget or off allowlist is recorded evidence. Deny means no merchant settlement.

We are live on Arbitrum Sepolia. SessionPolicy is deployed. Judges can open Executed and Denied transactions on Arbiscan.

Why Arbitrum: cheap session micropayments, and the same EVM policy maps later to ERC-4337 Smart Sessions.
